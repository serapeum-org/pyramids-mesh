PLY driver test fixtures.

bunny_res4.ply
  A real Stanford PLY: the Stanford Bunny reconstruction at its coarsest zipper
  resolution (res4) -- 453 vertices, 948 triangular faces, ASCII format. Each vertex
  carries extra 'confidence' and 'intensity' properties beyond x/y/z, which the driver
  must surface as node-located variables rather than drop. Small enough to commit
  (34 KB, well under the 5 MB pre-commit large-file cap) while being a genuine scanner
  mesh with real winding and coordinates.
    Source:  The Stanford 3D Scanning Repository, Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory
             http://graphics.stanford.edu/pub/3Dscanrep/bunny.tar.gz
             (bunny/reconstruction/bun_zipper_res4.ply)
    License: freely available for research/education; acknowledgement of the Stanford
             Computer Graphics Laboratory requested.

face_property_before_list.ply
  Hand-written negative fixture: a per-face scalar property ('property uchar intensity')
  declared on the face element before the vertex-index list, the exact header layout of
  the real Stanford Armadillo PLY. The driver refuses it (FileFormatNotSupportedError),
  since a trailing/leading per-face scalar would mis-align the face rows.

Larger real meshes (dragon, happy buddha, Lucy, and the full bunny) are NOT committed --
they exceed the 5 MB cap. They live in the gitignored examples/data/real-data/ply/ for
local performance/scaling checks; see that folder's SOURCE.txt.
